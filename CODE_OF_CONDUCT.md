# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
Maintainers may replace this text with the official Contributor Covenant at any time.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation. Contributors come from many countries and backgrounds; please
write clearly and assume good faith, especially across languages.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient and constructive; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Give and accept feedback graciously, especially in code and statistical review.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy: never share other people's personal information or real participant data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling or insulting comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this
Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem
inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **[INSERT CONTACT METHOD]**. All reports
will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when an individual represents the project in public.
